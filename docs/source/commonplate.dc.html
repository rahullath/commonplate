For the riders' co-op

Every trip on the record.

Gig work is hard to organise because it is hard to prove. Here each ride makes a receipt and each receipt adds to a work history the rider holds.

Pay formula is posted. The pool votes to change it.
A written reason before anyone is removed, and a panel that answers.
A small slice of each fare goes to a fund the riders run.
With a record of earnings and hours, riders can enrol in welfare boards, get group cover and bargain as one body. That is the step towards registration.
3
For kitchen workers

A vote and a voice.

Cooks, dishwashers and counter staff are members of the co-op too. They vote on the rules that touch their pay and hours, and they can raise a problem without the owner seeing who sent it.

Each rule lists who is allowed to change it.
Secret ballots. The result is public, the voter is not.
Tips and wage records are logged per order.
Kitchens, riders and staff each hold a third of the vote on shared matters. Pay and dispatch rules can only be changed by the people they apply to.

what it is not
Not a marketplace. The directory is a list and each kitchen links to its own page. Not a cart that mixes restaurants. Not a way to take a cut. Food money goes to the kitchen, the fare goes to the rider, and the co-op charges flat fees.
