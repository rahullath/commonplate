# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome.

- Keep changes small and focused. Explain the problem, not just the diff.
- Run the app and `python tests/smoke.py` before opening a pull request.
- Do not add a business's name, contact details, keys or customer data anywhere in the repository.
  Anything specific to one business belongs in configuration, not code.
- By contributing you agree your work is released under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
